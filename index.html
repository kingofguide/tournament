Tournament Rules & Guidelines

Official rules, scoring system, and clarifications

Goal

Battle and evolve to claim 1st place podium.

Rules

Drafting:
  • To begin, players select a monster from the pool.
  • After each round, players may draft a different monster from the pool.
  • If two or more players ever want the same monster, they roll 1d6 each. Highest wins, see Faces under Clarifications.
Modes:
  • Select one of the modes for the tournament.
    • Survival Mode:
      • Once a player wins, they finish their turn, claim first place and stop playing.
      • Repeat until second and third places are claimed, then the game is over.
    • Standard Mode:
      • Once a player wins, they finish their turn, claim first place and the game is over.
      • Second and third places are determined by VPs + Health, highest wins. In case of ties, the closest player in turn order to the winner wins.
Play:

Play each game as per normal rules except the following:

  • Must be played with the Power Up expansion.
  • To determine who goes first, each player rolls 1d6 at the same time. Highest wins, see Faces under Clarifications. In case of ties, repeat until one player wins.
Rounds:
  • Set the number of games per round, the recommended is 2.
  • Set the number of total rounds in the tournament.
  • Once a round is complete, the top seed players will move to the same table. This is repeated for the low seed players.
Scoring:
Places
  • 1st Place: 3 pts
  • 2nd Place: 2 pts
  • 3rd Place: 1 pt
  • 4th-6th Place: 0 pts
Bonuses
  • Smasher: 1 pt (most kills)
  • Tanker: 1 pt (most health)
  • Collector: 1 pt (most cards)
  • Harvester: 1 pt (most energy)

Clarifications

Faces: 1, 2, and 3 equal their numbers, Energy is 4, Hearts are 5, and then a Smash equals 6.
Places: In games where 2nd and/or 3rd places are not claimed, those points are not awarded.
Bonuses: Awarded to players at the end of the game.
  • Smasher: Granted to the player with the most kills. A kill is when an enemy is eliminated, the active player is awarded that kill.
  • Tanker, Collector, or Harvester: Granted to the player who has the most Health, Power Cards, or Energy, respectively. In case of ties, each player is awarded the bonus.
Expansions: The Power Up must be used, all other expansions are up to the tables.
Cards:
  • It's recommended to have a unique deck of Power cards per table. If this isn't possible, simply divide the single deck evenly between tables.

Reset Tournament?

Generate a New Tournament?

A tournament is already underway. Generating a new one will replace it, and all current rounds, games, scores and standings will be lost. Your player roster and settings will be kept.
