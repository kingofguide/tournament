Tournament Rules & Guidelines

Rules, scoring and clarifications

Goal

Battle to claim first place podium.

Guides: Find Monster guides at kingofguide.github.io/tokyo

Rules

Play
  • Play each game as per normal rules except the following:
  • The Power Up expansion must be used.
  • A maximum of 8 dice is allowed per player despite monster abilities.
  • To determine who goes first, each player rolls 1d6 at the same time. Highest wins, see Faces under Clarifications. In case of ties, repeat until one player wins.
Drafting
  • Players select one monster from the pool.
  • After each round, players may draft a different monster from the pool.
  • If two or more players ever want the same monster, they roll 1d6 each. Highest wins, see Faces under Clarifications.
Rounds
  • Set the number of games per round, the recommended is 1-2.
  • Set the number of total rounds in the tournament, recommended is 2-3.
  • Once a round is complete, the top seed players will move to the same table. This is repeated for the low seed players.

Scoring

  • Once a player is about to win, they finish their turn, claim first place and the game is over.
  • Second and third places are determined by VPs, highest wins. In case of ties, they roll 1d6 each. Highest wins, see Faces under Clarifications.
  • If a player is eliminated (0 HP), their VPs are set to 0 and they finish last.
  • All the players places, VPs and HPs should be entered into the Manager App.
  • Places: 1st = 3pts, 2nd = 2pts, 3rd = 1pt, 4th-6th = 0pt
  • Bonuses (optional): Smasher = 1pt (most kills), Tanker = 1pt (most health), Collector = 1pt (most cards), Harvester = 1pt (most energy)

Clarifications

  • Faces: 1, 2, and 3 equal their numbers, Energy is 4, Hearts are 5, and then a Smash equals 6.
  • Bonuses: optional points awarded to players at the end of the game.
  • Smasher: granted to the player with the most kills, a kill is when an enemy is eliminated, the active player is awarded that kill.
  • Tanker, Collector, or Harvester: granted to the player who has the most Health, Power Cards, or Energy, respectively at the end state of the game. In case of ties, they roll 1d6 each. Highest wins, see Faces under Clarifications.
  • Expansions: the Power Up must be used, all other expansions are up to the Tournament Managers.
  • Cards: It's recommended to have an identical deck of Power cards per table. If this isn't possible, simply divide the deck evenly between tables.

General

  • Rules: players should always follow the rules to their utmost ability.
  • Missed abilities: in case a player forgets to enforce a power or misses something, it's up to the active player, with help from the table, to decide if they can or not. In general, a fair chance should be given where possible as a fun atmosphere should be the priority over simply gaining an edge.
  • Cheating: in cases where players are cheating, the table decides their punishment. Anything from missing turns, losing VPs and/or disqualification can be taken, the imagination is endless.
  • Tips and Tricks: Follow the dice, let your first roll guide your turn. Follow your first Power Up, let it guide your play style. Go for Power Up cards and Power cards early to build up, but watch what your opponents are doing.

Disclaimer

  • The Power Tournament format is a work in progress and can be updated at any time, including during live games.
  • Decisions will be made by the Tournament Managers and are final.
  • Feedback is welcome, please email kingofguide(at)outlook(dot)com.

A note from the creator

Welcome fellow Kaiju battler,

I'm the creator of this Tournament Manager. It's a fun project I've been working on leading up to a tournament with friends.

While writing scores down on paper and organising brackets in real time is doable. I'd really rather not. Especially if I'm playing, it's the last thing I want to manage.

Enter this app! Where it does all that for me... and more.

Thanks to Claude, I vibe coded this sucker in no time. But my brain has got the best of me. "What if I add this? What if I make that?"

So welcome to my current obsession.

Just a note on the tournament system as it's a bit of everything. I'm calling it a hybrid tournament as I think it combines elimination-like brackets with swiss-like pairing. The thinking is to keep all players included while still building towards a pressure cooker finals. I love King of Tokyo, but I don't love that elimination games leave people out. So I didn't want a whole tournament isolating players. This is a casual approach to a competitive tournament. I hope it works!
